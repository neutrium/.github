# Neutrium

[Neutrium](https://neutrium.net) is an engineering knowledge base, centred mainly around chemical engineering design challenges faced by engineers in their daily work. We created Neutrium to bridge the gap between theory and practice.

In addition to our published articles we maintain a number of engineering focused Open-source libraries to bring our experience to your software project.

## Explore our work

Browse our [repositories](https://github.com/orgs/neutrium/repositories) for libraries, documentation, and examples.

Visit [neutrium.net](https://neutrium.net) for engineering articles and reference material.

## Get involved

Bug reports, feature suggestions, documentation improvements, and pull requests are welcome. Start with the issues and contribution guidance in the repository you’re interested in.

When reporting a calculation issue, include a reproducible example, the expected result, and any relevant reference.
